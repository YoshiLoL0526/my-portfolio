TopSite AI is a platform that generates and manages websites with AI, designed as a more controlled alternative to Shopify or Webflow.

- JWT auth, FREE/BASIC/PRO/ENTERPRISE plans and per-resource permissions.
- AI-only page creation and editing, with history and rollback between versions.
- Per-site management panel for content, products and campaigns.
- Architecture ready for payment methods and Sentry observability.
- Python/FastAPI backend, PostgreSQL and OpenAI; Gunicorn deployment.

Freelance project; no public repository.
