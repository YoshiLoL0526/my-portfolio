Synopsia is a team knowledge platform that turns documents, audio and folders into a searchable RAG knowledge base.

- Folder, document and audio management across multiple formats.
- Semantic search and answers over the content, with generated mind maps.
- Flask REST API with PostgreSQL, JWT, OTP and WauSMS notifications.
- OpenAI assistants for document queries, categories and mind maps.
- Logging, validation, Swagger docs and Docker deployment.

I worked on the backend and AI integration during my time at Synapse Innovation Lab; it is client work and has no public repository.
