InvokeIA is a platform for clinics that brings day-to-day management and AI automation into one product. It is my main engagement right now.

## Modules

- Scheduling and appointment management, with per-professional availability and reminders.
- Clinical records, treatment plans and patient follow-up.
- Sales, quotes, invoicing and payments.
- Automation workflows and a rules engine.
- Patient communications over WhatsApp and email.
- Results dashboards and activity reports.
- AI agents for reception, lead capture and follow-up.
- DICOM viewer for X-rays and CT scans.

## Technologies

Next.js and React on the frontend; n8n as the backend and automation engine; Python and FastAPI as the event server; PostgreSQL for data; Docker for deployment. The platform integrates with the WhatsApp Business API and other communication services.

The product is in production. The public site is invokeia.com; the code and internal details are not published.
