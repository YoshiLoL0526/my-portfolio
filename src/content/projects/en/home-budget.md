Home Budget is a mobile app for running a household budget across currencies: expenses, categories, budgets and reports on a local database.

- Income and expense tracking by category and period.
- Multi-currency support and conversion for expenses in different currencies.
- Summary and reports to see where the money goes.
- React Native + Expo with local SQLite and TypeScript.

A personal project to practise mobile product work and financial data modeling.
